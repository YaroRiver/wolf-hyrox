# THURSDAY 27 AUG — GATE 1: 5 KM CONTROLLED NEGATIVE-SPLIT BENCHMARK

**Gate condition: does not proceed until Monday's physician contact is confirmed done.**

## Warm-up
- **2 km easy**
- 3 × 20 sec relaxed strides
- 60–75 sec easy between
- 2 min reset

## Benchmark rule

**km 1 must be the slowest kilometre of the five.**
**km 5 must be faster than km 1.**

No all-out final 200 m. A sprint finish invalidates the benchmark for comparison purposes.

### Execution framework
- km 1: deliberately controlled
- km 2: settle
- km 3: build only if smooth
- km 4: hold / gently progress
- km 5: strongest controlled kilometre, mechanically clean

### Scoring
Primary score:
- **total 5 km time / average pace**

Validity criteria:
- km 1 = slowest split
- km 5 faster than km 1
- no final-200 m sprint
- mechanics remain clean

Secondary score:
- **negative split size: km 1 minus km 5**

This avoids gaming the benchmark by intentionally jogging km 1 just to create a large negative split.

### Record
- each km split
- HR each km
- average / peak HR
- cadence
- power
- RPE
- breathing
- final-km mechanics

## Cooldown
- **1 km very easy**

**Running: ~8 km total**
